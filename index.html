Booking Policies

♡

Please read before booking — these keep every appointment sweet and on time.

01

Advance Booking

All appointments must be booked at least 24 hours in advance through our Acuity scheduling system.

02

Walk-Ins

Walk-ins are not accepted unless stated otherwise.

03

Deposit

A $20 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking. This amount will be applied toward your total service cost.

04

Feeling Unwell

If you are feeling unwell or have any contagious condition, please contact us immediately to reschedule.

05

Rescheduling

Please reschedule at least 24 hours in advance.

06

No Call, No Show

All no call no shows will be banned from booking and charged 50% of the missed service.

07

Payment

We accept payments via cash or tap to pay. The remaining balance is due at the end of your service.

08

Service Changes

Please update or change your service selection at least 24 hours before your appointment to ensure proper time allocation.

09

Grace Period

There will be a 10 minute grace period for all appointments. If you are more than 10 minutes late, you will need to reschedule.
